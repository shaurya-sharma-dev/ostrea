Our Mission

Ostrea is an oyster-restoration education project created by siblings Shreya and Shaurya, students in Henrico County Public Schools. Our project began with a simple question: How can students in Henrico participate in Chesapeake Bay restoration even though they live far from the coast? We are building a website that allows students to follow a real oyster-restoration project from their classroom and learn about the Chesapeake Bay through direct observation, data, and hands-on activities.

The heart of Ostrea is a classroom "adoption" program centered around a real oyster cage hosted by our partner organization, the Tidewater Oyster Gardeners Association (TOGA) and its Oyster Master Gardeners. Students will follow the oysters throughout a short program through photographs, field observations, and data such as growth stage, water temperature, and site conditions. The website will connect these observations to lessons about oysters, ecosystems, water quality, and Chesapeake Bay restoration, allowing students to see how what they learn in the classroom relates to a real ecosystem.

Our mission is to make Chesapeake Bay restoration something that Henrico students can actively participate in, rather than simply learn about from a textbook. Through Ostrea, we hope to give students a direct connection to the Bay while introducing them to environmental science, data collection, and community-based restoration. We want to show students that they can contribute to protecting the Chesapeake Bay, even from miles away.
